What the score means

The system combines junction priority and hospital accessibility indicators to produce an emergency-access risk score.

Higher scores indicate locations that should receive greater attention during emergency-access planning.

The model is a decision-support prototype and should be validated using real traffic, emergency-response and incident data before operational deployment.
